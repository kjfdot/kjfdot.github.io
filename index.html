/ about me/əˈbaʊt/

I'm Kanij Fatema, a linguist from Bangladesh. I'm currently a Lecturer in the Department of English and Humanities at BRAC University. Before that, I was a Lecturer and course coordinator (ENG101) in the Department of English and Modern Languages at Independent University, Bangladesh. I did my BA and MA in Linguistics at the University of Dhaka.

I've also built datasets for a range of domain-specific work on the Bangla language. You can find them on my GitHub.

I'm hoping to broaden my computational linguistics horizons through grad school and more research work, so that language technology becomes accessible and inclusive for people, and not only something for companies to automate.

In my free time, I've been tinkering with Python, reading psychological crime thrillers, solving sudokus, chasing the chessboard, and hopping between cafés in Dhaka and wherever else I get to visit. Oh, and I'm testing the waters with cooking too.

fieldwork · Khagrachari

Nominal Morphology of Kokborok

I travelled to Khagrachari with my MA team to document Kokborok's nominal morphology with community members. Later, I returned to the language as Principal Investigator of an IUB Startup Research Fund study.

  • 2023–25

    Regional Bengali speech & dialect ASR

    I curated a linguistic framework for the annotation design of 10 regional dialects and validated the 68.6k-sample RegSpeech12 corpus, built to test ASR models and how well they handle dialectal variation in low-resource languages. The paper on this 78-hour speech corpus was accepted at IJCNLP–AACL 2025.

    RegSpeech12 on Kaggle ↗

  • 2024

    Bengali IPA transcription

    Bangla lacks automatic IPA transcription rules and models. With the help of a prominent linguistics expert of Bangladesh, I developed a structured, rule-based IPA framework that resolves a decade-long dispute. It aims to make language annotation easier for both standard and dialectal Bangla, and to make language algorithms accessible for foundation models.

    Dual IPA dataset on Kaggle ↗

  • 2023–24

    Indic Unicode normalization

    I gave the linguistic input on grapheme structure across seven Indic scripts.

  • 2024

    Mapping Violence dataset

    Bangla can get messy when it is (mis)used on social media. I oversaw the linguistic structure, provided the framework for annotation, and did some of the annotation myself to develop this 13,000-sentence dataset.

    Dataset on Hugging Face ↗

  • Bengali.AI

    Grammar error detection & correction (GED/GEC)

    A dataset of 100,000+ Bangla sentences. I helped develop the verb tag set and the annotation design, did around 40k annotations and a few more validations (good times!). We also ran a public GED competition on Kaggle with BUET in 2023. The morphological variation benchmark is part of this work: a corpus of tense, aspect, and morphological variation across standard and regional Bengali.

    Dataset on Hugging Face ↗

  • Bengali.AI

    Sylheti Nagri OCR dataset

    The preliminary dataset was built from age-old scripts collected by SUST students. I helped transcribe the Sylheti–Bangla–IPA framework and validated two datasets curated by 2–3 students.

    Dataset on GitHub ↗
